## Core Florida Biohazard & Environmental Remediation Protocols

1. **[Unattended Death & Decomposition Cleanup](https://alphabioclean.com/services/unattended-death-cleanup/)** — Biological fluid extraction, subfloor remediation, and hydroxyl/ozone odor neutralization.
2. **[Crime Scene Cleanup](https://alphabioclean.com/services/crime-scene-cleanup/) & [Suicide Cleanup](https://alphabioclean.com/services/suicide-cleanup/)** — Post-investigation bloodborne pathogen decontamination (OSHA 29 CFR 1910.1030).
3. **[Biohazard Remediation](https://alphabioclean.com/services/biohazard-remediation/) & [Blood Cleanup](https://alphabioclean.com/services/blood-cleanup/)** — Regulated medical waste removal and ATP clearance verification.
4. **[Compassionate Hoarding Cleanup](https://alphabioclean.com/services/hoarding-cleanup/) & [Odor Removal](https://alphabioclean.com/services/odor-removal/)** — Level 1–5 hoarding remediation, biohazard sanitization, and senior support.
5. **[Mold Remediation](https://alphabioclean.com/services/mold-removal/), [Water Damage Restoration](https://alphabioclean.com/services/water-damage-restoration/) & [Sewage Cleanup](https://alphabioclean.com/services/sewage-cleanup/)** — Negative-air HEPA containment and Category 3 black water decontamination.
